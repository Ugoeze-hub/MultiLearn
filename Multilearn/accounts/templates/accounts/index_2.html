Frequently Asked Questions

What is MyLearnQuest?

MyLearnQuest is a discovery platform that helps you find the best learning resources across multiple platforms. We don't create courses - we help you discover existing high-quality content.

How does MyLearnQuest help me learn?

We save you time by recommending the best free and paid courses from platforms like YouTube, Udemy and Coursera based on your learning goals and preferences.

Does MyLearnQuest offer certificates?

While we don't issue certificates ourselves, we can help you find certificate programs offered by platforms like Coursera and Udemy that match your needs.

Are the learning resources free?

We help you find both free resources (like YouTube tutorials) and premium courses. You can filter by price to find options that fit your budget.

Can I track my learning progress?

While we don't host courses, we can recommend platforms with excellent progress tracking features and help you find resources that match your learning pace.

How can I contact support?

Our team is available to help you find the best learning resources. Contact us through email or the contact form for personalized recommendations.
