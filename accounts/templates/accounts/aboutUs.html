At MyLearnQuest, we believe learning should be seamless, personalized, and boundary-free. Born from a passion for education technology, our platform bridges the gap between scattered online resources and structured learning—helping you discover, track, and master new skills effortlessly.

OUR STORY

Founded in 2024, MyLearnQuest began as a student project to solve a simple problem: "Why waste time jumping between platforms when you could learn from all of them in one place?"

Today, we've grown into an intuitive dashboard that aggregates top-tier courses from YouTube, Udemy, and beyond—paired with smart progress tracking to keep learners motivated, focused, and always moving forward.
